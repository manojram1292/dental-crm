# Launch Plan — Mahabharatam, As Vyasa Told It

## The promise (our north star)
Tell the Mahabharata **as the ancient text tells it**: condensed, never overhyped, never manipulated.
Where a beloved tale comes from later tradition, we say so on screen. If viewers ask for more depth,
we go deeper with long-form episodes that cover the text as it is.

Every decision below is checked against that promise. Growth tactics that break it are rejected.

---

## 1. Launch date
**Recommended: Saturday 19 December 2026, Gita Jayanti.**
- It is the day the Gita was spoken on Kurukshetra, a natural moment for a Mahabharata series.
- It falls on a weekend.
- It gives six weeks to build a buffer and run the pre-launch campaign.
- Panchangs differ between 19 and 20 Dec (Drik Panchang says the 19th). Confirm for your city.

**Launch-day drop:**
- the Intro/vision video (pinned), plus Episodes 1–3, so new visitors can binge;
- after that, **one episode a day** at a fixed time;
- start at 7–8 pm IST. Neither platform publishes a "best time", so we adjust from our own data after 2 weeks.

**Buffer rule:** never launch with less than 30 days of episodes in the bank. We have 20 now and are
making 21–30. The target is **episodes 1–50 finished by 19 Dec**, so a missed production day never
breaks the daily chain.

## 3. Pre-launch campaign (6 weeks: 5 Nov → 18 Dec)

**Week 1: Set up**
- Claim the handles.
- Write the bios (use the promise line above).
- Set the profile image (Sauti's fire mark) and a channel banner.
- Create the YouTube playlists:
  - Season 1;
  - one per arc;
  - the Intro.
- Connect the scheduling/analytics tool (section 5).

**Weeks 2–5: Build curiosity.** Three posts a week, all true and all respectful:
1. **Character reveals**: a card with a one-line who's-who, cut from our character bible. Ganesha, Vyasa, Garuda, Vishnu and others.
2. **"What the text actually says"**: short facts where the Critical Edition differs from popular memory. Examples:
   - the enchantress is never named Mohini in the epic;
   - the tortoise holding the mountain is Akupara, the tortoise king.

   Never mock the TV versions. The frame is always "the text says…, tradition adds…".
3. **Behind the scenes**: "every frame is drawn in code", with a timelapse of a scene building. Say plainly that the voices are synthetic.
4. **15-second trailer cuts** of the strongest moments: the horse rising, the Blue Throat, Garuda's birth.

**Week 6: Countdown**
- The trailer (the Intro video).
- "Starts on Gita Jayanti", plus a reminder post on each platform.
- Seed your own circle honestly: family, friends, community groups, temples and Sanskrit or reading groups you're part of.

**Never:** buy followers, views or bots; use engagement-bait ("comment 🙏 or bad luck"); make misleading thumbnails; or make claims we can't support.

**Collaboration (from week 3):** list 10 creators in mythology, Sanskrit, Indian history and storytelling, and send each a short, genuine note with a sample episode. Offer a "who's who" carousel they can share. Expect a few replies, not many.

## 6. Continuous improvement: how quality goes up every episode

**Before launch (no audience data yet): three independent checks**
1. **Self-review**: after every render, what worked, what's weak, and the fix carried forward → `REVIEW_LOG.md`.
2. **Measured scorecard**: hard numbers per episode, not opinions → `QUALITY_SCORECARD.md`.
   - Hook text on screen at frame 0.
   - Longest static shot ≤ 4 s.
   - Mid-point turn at 40–60%.
   - 0 caption overlaps on key figures.
   - 0 repeated compositions versus the last 3 episodes.
   - A fidelity reference to the text.
3. **Independent critic**: every 10 episodes, a separate reviewer that did not make them scores each one and lists the top fixes → `review/`. The production side must adopt each fix or write down why not.

**After launch: a weekly loop driven by audience data**
- **Read the numbers**, per episode:
  - viewed vs swiped away (first 3 s);
  - average % viewed;
  - rewatches/loops;
  - **sends/shares**;
  - saves;
  - follows per 1,000 views;
  - comments.
- **Find** the 2 best and 2 worst episodes of the week, and why: the hook, the topic, the pacing or the ending.
- **Change at most 2 things** for next week's episodes, so we know what caused what.
- **Test** big hook ideas on Instagram Trial Reels (two versions, keep the winner).
- **Record** it all in `PERFORMANCE_LOG.md`: what changed, why, and whether it worked.
- **Never** trade fidelity for numbers. If a "winning" idea needs exaggeration, we don't use it.
