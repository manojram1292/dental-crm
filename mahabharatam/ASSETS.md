# ASSETS: everything used in the films, its source and its licence

Short version: **every picture is drawn by our own code, every note of music and every sound effect is synthesised by our own
code.** The only outside material is listed below. No stock footage, stock music, samples, photos or AI-generated images are in
any finished film. No paid service was used for any asset.
