## The pace

Introductions 3.4–4.2 s each (the trailer's ran 2.1–3.0 s and the owner felt it "running"); the war run's shots 3.6–4.4 s but
cut against a drum engine at 120–132 bpm, hits on every cut and arrows crossing the frame; the peak is the one long hold (5.7 s).
Every camera moves; no picture repeats; nothing is shown twice.
