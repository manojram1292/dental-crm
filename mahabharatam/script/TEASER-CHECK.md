# War teaser · fidelity check against the Critical Edition

Pipeline number 96, script `script/teaser.en.json` (kind "trailer", "teaser": 1). The shot plan is `script/TEASER-PLAN.md`.

**Text read:** the GRETIL e-text of the BORI Critical Edition, one file per book (`lab/ce/mbh_NN_u.htm`, git-ignored). Each verse below was read for this teaser with `python3 -I lab/review/teaser/ce.py ref BOOK CH V0 V1`. Only lines without `*` (star passage) or `@` (appendix) count as the constituted text.

**What this file is, and what it is not:**
- These are readings of plain verses. The Sanskrit is quoted so that someone who reads it can check each one.
- I am not a Sanskritist.
- Books 4 and 12–16 were not opened.

**Rule:** every spoken word and every picture rests on a verse listed here. Anything else is marked **not in the verses** (staging), or it was cut.

## The promise: what is claimed, and why it is true as worded

The owner asked that viewers know the series "is taken from the text verse by verse, as the scholars read it". The series condenses (about a minute an episode), so "every verse" would not be true. What was checked:

| wording | used? | check |
|---|---|---|
| "Taken from the Critical Edition, the text scholars read." | **spoken and captioned** | The scripts name the BORI Critical Edition as their source (`script/epNN.en.json` "source"; checked for 01–60). The CE is the edition used in scholarship. |
| "Nothing added for effect." | **spoken** | The series' standing rule. Later-tradition material is left out or tagged "As tradition tells it" (episode 14 is tagged so). |
| "Every episode cites its verses." | **NOT used** | Checked on 2026-11-16. Episodes 41–60 show a source line on screen (`strings.sourceLine`, for example "Adi Parva 1.67 · BORI Critical Edition"). Episodes 01–40 do not: their scripts name the chapters in "source", but nothing is shown on screen. The claim is false for 01–40 as they stand. |
| "Told in order." | **NOT used** | `SERIES.md` records three moves for continuity (the trailer's check gives them). |
| "Verse by verse" | **NOT used** | The series condenses. |

What the teaser shows instead is its own practice. Every plaque carries its verse, and the promise shot lays down the references of the ten verses this film rests on. That is true of this film, and it shows the method.

## Cut, or left out

- **Bhima** ("Bhima has sworn to break Duryodhana's thigh", 2.63.14, verified and voiced) and a separate **Vyasa shot** for "Nothing added for effect": cut for length (the film ran 72 s with them). "Nothing added for effect" is now the second sentence of the promise line.

- **Bhishma's fall, the boy alone, Karna's wheel** (the trailer's war beats): left out. The teaser stops at the universal form, before the fighting, where the text has it. The war is promised by the eighteen armies (1.2.234) and by "I am Time".
- **"Eighteen days of war"**: true (1.2.234), but left out for length. The war run ends on the peak.
- **Astras:** no named divine weapon is used before 6.33, so none is shown. The blasts and the light in this film are the form's light (6.33.12, 17), drawn with the same 3D tools as the effects an astra would use.
- **Duryodhana's banner (the serpent), Arjuna's bow by name (Gandiva), the number of Arjuna's horses:** not read for this film, and not said. The banners of the hosts are plain pennants. The chariot's banner carries the monkey (6.23.20).
