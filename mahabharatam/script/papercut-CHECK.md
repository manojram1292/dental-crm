# Paper-cut sample (pipeline 95) · verse check

The birth of Gandhari's hundred sons, told from the BORI Critical Edition, Adi parvan chapter 107 (CE 1.107), GRETIL e-text of the
constituted text (`lab/ce/adi.txt`, lines `01,107.*`). Only the constituted verses (a/c/e lines) are used. The star passages (`*1135`
to `*1150`) and appendix 63 are apparatus: lines found only in some manuscripts. They are listed at the end because several familiar
details come from them and are therefore left out.

## Left out because it is not in the constituted text

- Duryodhana braying like a donkey at birth, donkeys, vultures and crows answering, winds and fires in the sky (`*1142`).
- A rain of blood and the carnivores' howl "as Duryodhana was born" (`*1143`; the CE's own howl, 28, comes at the end of the king's question).
- Bhima born on the same day as Duryodhana (`*1142`).
- Duryodhana "dark, with tawny eyes" who will end the kshatriyas (`*1147`).
- Gandhari wishing for a daughter, and Vyasa making the hundred-and-first part for her (appendix 63).
- Pandu's grief while Gandhari was pregnant (`*1135`).
- "Two years" before the pots are opened: the CE says only "after so much time".
- Striking her womb with an iron bar (later retellings; the CE: *pātayām āsa … yatnena mahatā*).

Also not used, though constituted: Yuyutsu, son of the vaishya woman (1.107.35–36); the brahmins' proverb "give up one for the family,
the family for the village …" (32).
