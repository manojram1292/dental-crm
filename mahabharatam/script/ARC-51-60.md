# Episodes 51–60 · the story plan

The plan, the verse check and the scripts for the batch after episode 50, written before any of these episodes was voiced. Scripts: `script/ep51.en.json` … `ep60.en.json`, generated by `tools/write_scripts_51_60.py` (the words live there until an episode is voiced; after that the JSON is the source). It mirrors `script/ARC-41-50.md`: the same rules (its §4), the same staging rules (its §9.2), and the faults the independent critic found there (`review/independent-41-50-scripts.md`) were used as a checklist here. The adversarial self-read is in `review/self-51-60-scripts.md`.

Three arcs meet in this batch:

- **51–52 · Vyasa Begins, the last two** (catalog S01A07, main line; CE Adi 1.58 and 1.61). Episode 52 is an arc finale.
- **53–58 · Shakuntala** (catalog S01A08, a side arc; CE Adi 1.62–69). Six episodes, one finale (58).
- **59–60 · Yayati, the first two: the tale of Kacha** (catalog S01A09, a side arc; CE Adi 1.71). The arc runs on to episode 70.

## 1. Where the batch picks up, and what it hands on

Episode 50 ends on the king's question (CE 1.58.1–2): why were these godlike heroes born on earth? Episode 51 answers it in its second line, in the teller's mouth, and then shows how; 52 names who was born as whom and closes the arc. 53 opens the family's own story where the text opens it (1.62.2–3): with Dushyanta. 58 closes Shakuntala and hands to the king's actual next question (1.71.1): how did Yayati win the daughter of Shukra; 59–60 tell the tale the text tells first in answer: Kacha.

## 3. Where the catalog plan was wrong (corrected in `catalog/series.json`)

1. **60, title and synopsis**: "Kacha Dies Three Times" → **"Kacha Dies Twice"** (CE 1.71.26, 33; the third death is 701*).
2. **52**: "Vidura of Dharma" is not in this chapter: CE 1.61.79 reads *atres tu … viduram* (a son of Atri). Vidura is left out. Balarama (Shesha), Bhishma (the Vasus) and Drona (Brihaspati) are in the chapter; Bhishma and Drona are on plaques, Balarama is not used.
3. **55, cliffhanger**: "Years passed. No army came." The CE never says so; it says she and the boy were still at the hermitage when he was six (1.68.5–10). Now: "Years later she was still in the forest, with a son who tied up lions."
4. **58, cliffhanger**: "Yayati had paid a terrible price for desire" belongs to 1.70.33–46 / 1.78–80, many episodes on, and episode 59 does not answer it. Now the king's own next question (1.71.1). Also dropped from the synopsis: "and the land" (not in 1.69.49).
5. **59, cliffhanger**: "The asuras saw through him": he was never in disguise (1.71.17: he names his father). Now: "the asuras understood why he had come" (1.71.25, buddhva).
6. **51, cliffhanger**: "the gods came down … as the heroes and villains": the villains are not gods (1.58.25–31, 1.61.4–61, 80). Now: "gods and demons were born as men".
7. **56, synopsis**: "lions, tigers and elephants" is right but short (1.68.5: tigers, lions, boars, elephants, buffalo); the king's words are "I do not remember" (1.68.18), "I do not know you" comes at 1.68.80 (episode 57's title).
8. **53–58 `refs`** were chapter ranges set before reading; they are now the chapters actually read.

## 4. Rules (as ARC-41-50 §4, with this batch's additions)

Hook ≤ 8 words, spoken by ≤ 3.0 s; story by 5 s; turn at 40–60 % after a real rest; ≤ 59 s (54–58 the target); ≤ 2.6 words/s; ≤ 2 new names per 10 s, each with an identifier; the previous episode's question answered by 15 s; one signature shot, one close-up with a change of emotion, ≥ 4 camera moves, ≥ 2 medium transitions, a palette on every line with no hue for more than two lines, ambience and foley on every line, ≥ 3 music moods, ≥ 2 J/L cuts; the source line and "Voices: synthetic · Drawn in code".

New in this batch:

- **No episode opens on fire** (41–43 all did). The ten first frames: the Earth under her burden; five men under five gods at dawn; a man's back in an empty yard; a ring of birds from above; a held-out hand at evening; a boy's back and a tied lion; a hand on a throne's arm; a hall's roof seen from the floor; a dead hand closing in green light; a dropped staff in a violet wood.
- **Finales** (52, 58): a lit payoff of at least 4 s and a silent arc-end card, as in episode 46.
- **Speeches are single verses.** Where a character speaks, the line is one verse (or two adjacent ones) in paraphrase, and from 57 on the verse number is on screen.
- **A name the voice cannot afford goes on a plaque** (the names-per-10-s rule): Indra in 54, Dushyanta in 54 and 56, Kali and Narayana in 52. A plaque is still a claim and was checked like a spoken line.
- **Staging** (ARC-41-50 §9.2, plus): a new-born is a swaddled infant (54, 56); Shakuntala is drawn slim once her son is born (56); the two in 53–55 stand three paces apart, and the joined hands of 55 `wed` are the only touch; in 58 the embrace is father and small child, kneel-and-carry; in 60 neither killing is drawn and Kacha comes out through light, not a wound; a god is always a whole large figure, never an emblem (51, 52).

## 6. Episode by episode

### Episode 51 · The Burden of the Earth

- **CE reference:** Adi Parva 1.58 (the script's `source` lists every verse).
- **Recap band (unvoiced):** Asked why godlike heroes were born on earth, the teller answered.
- **Hook (question):** *Why were gods born as men?* 6 words, spoken by 2.42 s. First frame: the Earth as a woman, large in the frame, bent under a dark mass that rests on her shoulders: a hill of tiny towers, spears and beasts. A tilt up from her bowed head to the mass. The card low.
- **Loop in:** "Why were godlike heroes born on earth?" Answered by line `secret` at 3.5 s: *"To lift a burden from the Earth. It is a secret of the gods."*
- **This episode's question:** "Why were gods born as men?" Answered by line `command` at 34.9 s: *"Gods: be born on earth, each in part, and lift her burden."*
- **Loop out:** "Which god was born as which hero?" Last line: *So gods and demons were born as men: this story's heroes and villains.*
- **The turn:** line `weight` (*The Earth could not bear her own weight.*) at 45 %, after a real silence. Rests before: `weight`, `agreed`.
- **Signature shot:** line `went`: a crane up and back: she climbs, small, a long stair of cloud toward a great seated four-faced figure on a lotus, large in the upper frame, ringed by rows of small bright gods (plaque: Brahma · the creator).
- **Face close-up:** line(s) `weight`.
- **Colour script, in order:** dharma-copper (18°) → satra-teal-night (192°) → meadow-spring (74°) → venom-violet (284°) → dice-vermilion (8°) → dharma-copper (18°) → memory-indigo (236°) → brahma-rose (338°) → sun-white (48°) → crystal-sky (196°) → hunt-emerald (128°). **New against episode 50:** dharma-copper, satra-teal-night, meadow-spring, venom-violet, dice-vermilion, memory-indigo, sun-white, crystal-sky, hunt-emerald.

- **Voices:** narrator, vaishampayana, brahma. **Names in the voice and on plaques:** Earth (the goddess who bears the world); Brahma (the creator); Narayana (Vishnu, the preserver); Parashurama (the axe-bearing sage); Indra (king of the gods); Vaishampayana (the teller, Vyasa's student); Janamejaya (the king who asked).
