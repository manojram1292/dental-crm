# Trailer · fidelity check against the Critical Edition

Text read: the GRETIL e-text of the BORI Critical Edition, one file per book (`lab/ce/mbh_NN_u.htm`, fetched from
gretil.sub.uni-goettingen.de; git-ignored). Only lines whose reference has no `*` (star passage) and no `@` (appendix)
were counted as the constituted text. I read the verses quoted below myself; I did not read whole chapters around every
one of them, and I am not a Sanskritist: each reading is of plain narrative verses, and the quotations are given so that
someone who is can check them. Books not opened at all: 4, 9 (opened only by search), 12–16, 18.

Rule applied: every image and every spoken word is in a verse listed here, or it was cut.

## Cut because of the check, or left out because it was not verified

- **"With one shot"** (the svayamvara): the text has five arrows. Reworded.
- **Krishna supplying Draupadi's cloth**: star passage (543*), not the constituted text. Not shown, not said.
- **Krishna throwing the wheel at Bhishma**: he raises it and is held back. The cold open shows exactly that; the release is the Shishupala scene, where the text has it.
- **Ekalavya**: I only located the chapter (Adi 1.123) and did not read the verses of the thumb. Cut.
- **Abhimanyu's age, the six warriors by name, the closing of the breach by Jayadratha**: not read. Not said.
- **Karna's curses (the brahmin's cow, Parashurama)**: 8.29.31 mentions the brahmin's word about the wheel; I did not read the passage. Not said.
- **The dog revealed as Dharma; Yudhishthira's test in heaven**: not read. Not said.
- **Bhima and Baka, the dice hall vow of Bhima, Draupadi's question to the hall, the year in disguise, Krishna's embassy and his cosmic form, Drona's death, Duryodhana's fall by the lake**: not in the trailer (length), not checked.

## The promise line

The brief asked for "nothing left out". The series condenses (about a minute an episode) and told the Bhrigu tales after the Astika tale, so the spoken promise is the one the episodes keep: "All of it, start to finish. Nothing added for effect."
