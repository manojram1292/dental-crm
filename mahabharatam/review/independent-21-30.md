## 3. Cross-episode patterns

- **New template.** All ten run the same sequence:
  - a caps hook on a dark top band, with gold keywords;
  - the title lockup at about 3 s in the same place;
  - the "Previously" plaque at the lower left;
  - the Sauti fire cut;
  - plaques;
  - the NEXT › band at about y = 1160;
  - a dissolve.

  Openings A, B and C differ only in grammar, rotate mechanically (A-B-C-A…), and look identical on screen.
- **Mass-produced risk.** One protagonist for 14 episodes, in two poses: the frontal spread-wing (hooks of 21 and 26, plus 28 and 30) and the dive (22 and 29 hooks, 21, 25 and 29 ends). The lights vary; the frame and the sprite don't.
- **Audio.** Loudness is solved. But LRA is 1.9–2.6 LU everywhere, and the gaps sit only about 10 dB under speech, so nothing breathes. Mother and son have different accents (Vinata British, Garuda American).
- **Character readability.** Garuda is fixed. Vinata, Kashyapa, Indra, Brihaspati, Vishvakarma and the navy Vishnu are still black cut-outs on bright grounds in 9 of 10 episodes. The new problem is scale: the Nishadas, the Valakhilyas, the shrunk Garuda and the serpents in 30 are under about 5 % of frame height.
- **Info-dumps:** the quarters in 26, two plaques within 4 s in 23.

## 4. Top 10 for episodes 31–40

1. **Give every speaker a lit fill and a readable face.** *Measure:* zero contact-sheet stills where the speaker's interior is below 20 % luminance on a ground above 60 %.
2. **Keep the named subject at 10 % of frame height or more**, or cut to an insert. *Measure:* bounding-box height on each line's midpoint still.
3. **Vary the frame, not the grammar.** Choose the hook card's position and style by content, vary when the title lands, and give each arc its own NEXT treatment. *Measure:* dHash distance between episodes on crops of the 0.5 s, 3 s and last frames, below the ep 21–30 baseline.
4. **Hook-copy rules:** no undefined pronoun, no stated payoff, no text over a deity's face. *Measure:* a checklist, plus a Trial Reel A/B on ep 31.
5. **Real silence and dynamics:** one rest at −45 dB or below for 0.6 s or more, and a stinger on the reveal. *Measure:* silencedetect finds 1 or more; LRA ≥ 4 LU.

## 5. Factual and respect concerns

- **Fidelity is the best yet.** The following match my recollection of CE Adi 1.23–30:
  - the wager won by deceit (1.23);
  - the brahmin and his Nishadi wife (1.24–25);
  - Vibhavasu and Supratika (1.25);
  - the leaf-stalk in the hoofprint, with Shaunaka asking (1.27);
  - Vishvakarma and the four quarters (1.28);
  - the 90 × 90 mouths, the wheel and the two serpents (1.29);
  - "above you" and the banner (1.29);
  - the feather and Suparna (1.29–30);
  - the kusha made holy (1.30).

  No tradition tag is needed in this run, and none was used. That is correct.
- **Ep 28.** The boon is faithful (1.29), but isolated as a hook it reads as hubris toward Vishnu. Try "A bird asked to stand above Vishnu", with the text kept off the deity's face.
- **Eps 21–22.** The red "eye" coal makes Garuda look demonic. Put it below the collar and give it a shape that can't be read as an eye.
- **Vinata.** Her hand-on-hip pose undercuts both her grief (21) and her release (30).
- **Catalog.** Ep 11 is `side` in a `main` arc with no `layers` split. Either add a split or make the validator allow it.
