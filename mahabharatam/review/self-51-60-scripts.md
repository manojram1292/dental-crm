# Episodes 51–60 · the writer's own adversarial read of the scripts

Done on the generated scripts (`tools/write_scripts_51_60.py` → `script/ep51…60.en.json`) before voicing, twice over: once as a hostile fact-checker with the constituted text open (`lab/ce/adi.txt`, star and appendix lines filtered out), once as a first-time viewer who has seen no other episode and hears the voice without reading the recap band. This is a self-review, not an independent one: the same hand wrote the lines. An independent critic has not read these scripts.

Checklist used: the faults the independent critic found in 41–50 (`review/independent-41-50-scripts.md`): an order of telling implied but not the text's; a line attributed to the wrong speaker; a detail from the vulgate presented as the text; a pronoun with no owner; a name never introduced; a hook whose question is answered in another episode; a cliffhanger that the next episode does not answer.

## C. What this read could not check

- How the lines sound: the read was of text. The voices were made afterwards (Kokoro); nobody but the writer has listened, and the writer listened to none of them end to end: lengths, rates and silences were measured, not heard.
- The Sanskrit pronunciations in `tools/voice.py` (written by rule, printed with `--dry`).
- Whether a viewer who knows the popular Shakuntala (the ring) or the three deaths of Kacha will take the on-screen notes as corrections or as mistakes: that needs a real audience.
